# CTPlay recipes

[← Back to the user guide](user-guide.md)

Short recipes to make something that sounds good straight away, one or two for each feature. The
[user guide](user-guide.md) explains every feature in full; the links go there.

Each recipe starts from a **new empty project** (C natural minor, as the Circuit Tracks starts) with **Play** pressed:
the pattern is empty and changes while it plays. The notes you should hear are given at each step, lowest first, so you
can check.

- **Pages:** Shift + a track's button opens its [Play pages](user-guide.md#play-pages). Then hold Shift and tap a pad
  on the bottom row: 25 Chords, 26 Arp, 27 Pattern tools, 28 Melody, 32 Keys. Tap 27 or 28 again to flip to Pattern
  tools 2 or Phrasing.
- **▼ / ▲** (the Octave buttons) also go to the next / previous page, in this order: Chords, Arp, Pattern tools,
  Pattern tools 2, Melody, Phrasing, Keys. The recipes say "(or ▼)" where that is a shortcut.
- **The drums' pages** are on Shift + Drum 1–4: hold Shift and tap 26 Repeat or 27 Pattern tools (tap 27 again for
  Pattern tools 2).
- **A page's pads:** after you open a page, let go of Shift. The pads named next are on the page itself.
- **Press Note** to close a page and play the pads.
- **Write** is pad 32: press it twice. When a recipe uses one of the newer settings (a groove, a cycle, an accent,
  ties, …), the Write pad then stays bright orange, and every press on Pattern tools or Melody re-writes the pattern
  when you lift your finger (the [draft](user-guide.md#the-draft)). Otherwise each Write takes two presses.
- **Undo** (pad 31) takes the pattern back to how it was before the first Write.

## Chords

### A chord progression that moves smoothly

Four chords played the plain way jump around the keyboard. With [voice leading](user-guide.md#voice-leading), each
chord takes the inversion closest to the one before, so the notes barely move.

Choose a soft pad or keys patch on Synth 1. You play the pads C, A♭, E♭, B♭ and C again, all from one octave: a C pad
and the pads above it up to B♭.

1. Shift + Synth 1 opens the Chords page (Shift + pad 25 in the page list). Pad 2: the triad.
2. Press Note and play the five pads, one after another. You hear C E♭ G, then A♭ C E♭, E♭ G B♭, B♭ D F and C E♭ G.
   Each chord starts on its pad, so the chords jump up and down.
3. Shift + Synth 1 opens the Chords page again. Let go of Shift and tap pad 25 (close voice leading).
4. Press Note and play the same pads again. You hear C E♭ G, then **C E♭ A♭**, E♭ G B♭, **D F B♭** and C E♭ G. Each
   chord keeps or shares notes with the one before: it sounds like one hand moving a little, not jumping.
5. Shift + Synth 1, then pad 26 (top line).
6. Press Note and play them again. Only the third chord changes: **B♭ E♭ G**. Now listen to the top notes: G, A♭, G,
   B♭, G. They make a small melody of their own.
7. To keep it: press Record and hold each chord for a beat (four steps). The chords record as you hear them.

To try next:

- Chords page pad 3 (the 7th): four notes per chord, and voice leading makes them move even more smoothly.
- Hold Shift in the Note view: the [Quick view](user-guide.md#quick-view) shows the chord shapes, so you can change
  the shape while you play. Voice leading stays as you set it on the Chords page.

### Off-beat chord stabs

Short chords on the off-beats, the stab of classic house. You enter them into steps with
[chords in step entry](user-guide.md#chords-in-step-entry), then strum them with
[pattern strum](user-guide.md#pattern-strum).

Choose a short, bright patch on Synth 1 (a pluck or a stab).

1. Shift + Synth 1 opens the Chords page (Shift + pad 25). Pad 3: the 7th.
2. Hold Shift, tap pad 32: the Keys page. Pad 2: bright green (chords in step entry on).
3. Press Note. The top two rows are the steps. Hold step 3 (pad 3) and press a C pad: you hear C E♭ G B♭ while you
   hold. Let go of both.
4. Do the same on step 7.
5. Hold step 11 and press the A♭ pad above that C: you hear A♭ C E♭ G. Let go of both.
6. Do the same on step 15.
7. The pattern plays the stabs between the beats: C minor 7 twice, then A♭ major 7 twice.
8. To take one out, hold its step and press the same pad again: hold step 7, press C. The step is empty. Put it back
   the same way.

**Strum them.**

9. Shift + Synth 1 opens the Keys page again (the page you left). Pad 3: Played (white).
10. Hold Shift, tap pad 25: the Chords page. Pad 30 (fast). Each stab now strums up, 20 ms between its notes, like a
    guitar's upstroke. Its notes still end together.
11. Pad 29 (medium) gives a looser strum. Pad 32 (falling) strums down, from the highest note.

To try next:

- Keys page (Shift + pad 32) pad 4 (Written): stabs you enter from now on go in as rolls, so they strum on the official
  firmware too. The stabs already in the pattern stay plain chords, and on Written they play unstrummed.
- Chords page pad 4 (the 9th): a fuller stab, five notes.

## Arpeggiator

### An arpeggio you can play over

A latched [arpeggio](user-guide.md#arpeggiator) keeps going on its own while you change its chord or play another
track.

Choose a pluck or a bell patch on Synth 1.

1. Shift + Synth 1 opens the Chords page (Shift + pad 25). Pad 3: the 7th.
2. Hold Shift, tap pad 26: the Arp page (or ▼).
3. Pad 2 (up), pad 11 (1/16).
4. Pad 18 (2 octaves), pad 16 (latch).
5. Press Note and tap a C pad once. You hear sixteenths: C E♭ G B♭, then the same an octave up, over and over. It
   goes on after you let go.
6. Tap the A♭ pad above it. The arpeggio changes to A♭ C E♭ G and the same an octave up.
7. Hold Shift and press 1-16/17-32 (it lights blue): the [Quick view](user-guide.md#quick-view) now shows the Arp
   page's top rows. Still holding Shift, tap pad 20 (up-down) or pad 22 (random). The arpeggio changes pattern as it
   plays. Shift + 1-16/17-32 again brings the chord shapes back.
8. Play another track: the arpeggio goes on.
9. To stop it: Shift + Synth 1 opens the Arp page (the page you left). Pad 16 (latch off).

To try next:

- Record it: press Record while it plays. At 1/16 the notes record as you hear them.
- Arp page pad 21 makes the notes shorter (each press one sixth of a step), down to 1/6, then legato.

## Melodies and bass lines

These use [Pattern tools](user-guide.md#euclidean-fills) for the rhythm and the
[melodic generator](user-guide.md#melodic-generator) for the notes.

### A melody from a 9th chord

Synth 2 holds a C minor 9th chord. Synth 1 plays a melody made from the same chord's notes, so the two always fit.

**The chord (Synth 2).** Choose a soft pad or keys patch.

1. Shift + Synth 2 opens the Chords page (Shift + pad 25). Pad 4: the 9th.
2. Hold Shift, tap pad 27: Pattern tools. Pad 1: one hit.
3. Pad 21 three times, until it is white: each note lasts until the next hit, here the whole bar.
4. Hold Shift, tap pad 28: Melody. Pad 18: degree 1 (C). Pad 26: octave −1.
5. Pad 32 twice. You hear C3 E♭3 G3 B♭3 D4 together, held for the whole bar.

**The melody (Synth 1).** Choose a pluck, lead or bell patch.

6. Shift + Synth 1 opens the Chords page (Shift + pad 25). Pad 4 (9th). The melody picks its notes from this chord.
   Synth 1's pads now also play 9th chords when you play them yourself.
7. Hold Shift, tap pad 27: Pattern tools. Pad 7 (7 hits), pad 20 (groove, bright white).
8. Pad 21 three times (white). Rows 1–2 show the hits: steps 1, 5, 8, 9, 12, 13 and 16.
9. Hold Shift, tap pad 28: Melody. Pad 4 (up-down), pad 12 (2 octaves), pad 18 (degree 1).
10. Pad 32 twice. You hear the chord climb: C4 E♭4 G4 B♭4 C5 D5 E♭5. It is an arpeggio, not a tune yet.
11. Pad 7 (random). When you lift your finger the line becomes C4 D5 C4 E♭4 D5 C4 D5: jumps between low C and high D.
    That is already a hook.
12. Hold Shift, tap pad 28 again: Phrasing (or ▼). Pad 2 ([ties](user-guide.md#ties) 1 in 3). Now you hear C4 D5, then
    C4 held from step 8 to step 13, then E♭4 D5. One long note between short ones makes the line sound sung.
13. Hold Shift, tap pad 28: back to Melody (or ▲). Pad 14 twice (white, [accent](user-guide.md#accents) on the beat).
    Steps 1, 5 and 13 play harder: the line leans on the beat.
14. Now roll the [dice](user-guide.md#dice). Pad 30 gives new notes (and new ties) on the same rhythm. Pad 29 gives a
    new groove for the same notes. Press until you like it. Undo goes back to the empty pattern, not to the previous
    roll.
15. Save the project (Save, Save) to keep the pattern and every setting.

To try next:

- Melody page pad 10 (pool: scale): the line also uses F and A♭, notes outside the chord. They add some tension. With
  the scale, pad 5 (climb) or 6 (walk) moves step by step.
- A 7th (Chords page pad 3) sounds plainer; sus2 or sus4 (pads 5, 6) sound open. Change it on both tracks: Synth 2's
  chord and Synth 1's notes come from the same shape.

### A bass line

A syncopated bass on Synth 2: the root on the strong beats, the fifth in between, octave jumps. Then a second chord.

1. Shift + Synth 2 opens the Chords page (Shift + pad 25). Leave the shape off (pad 1 bright). With the shape off, the
   bass picks from the triad: C E♭ G. Choose a bass patch.
2. Hold Shift, tap pad 27: Pattern tools. Pad 6 (6 hits). Rows 1–2 show steps 1, 4, 7, 9, 12 and 15: 3 + 3 + 2
   steps, twice. This is the tresillo, the pulse under a lot of pop, Latin and house music.
3. Pad 21 once: short notes (2/6 of a step).
4. Hold Shift, tap pad 28: Melody. Pad 8 (bass), pad 18 (degree 1).
5. Pad 25 (octave −2).
6. Pad 32 twice. You hear C2 G2 C2 C2 C2 G2.
7. Pad 14 twice (on the beat): steps 1 and 9 play harder.
8. Hold Shift, tap pad 28 again: Phrasing (or ▼). Pad 6 ([octave jumps](user-guide.md#octave-jumps) 1 in 3). Steps
   7, 9 and 12 jump up: C2 G2 C3 C3 C3 G2. The octave jump is the classic disco and house bass move.
9. Pad 30 (dice notes) for another line. Step 1 stays on C.

**A second chord.**

10. Press Note to close the pages. In stock's Patterns view, select Synth 2's pattern 2.
11. Shift + Synth 2 opens the page you left (Phrasing). Hold Shift and tap pad 28 to flip to Melody (or ▲).
12. Pad 23 (degree 6, A♭), then pad 32 twice (the draft ended when you closed the pages). You hear A♭2 E♭3 A♭3 A♭3
    A♭3 E♭3.
13. Chain patterns 1 and 2 in the Patterns view: a bar of C, a bar of A♭. The same rhythm, octave jumps and dice apply
    to both patterns, because the settings belong to the track.

### A riff that drifts against the beat

An arpeggio in groups of five steps over a 16-step bar, so each group starts at a different place in the bar. It is a
common trance and techno trick.

1. Shift + Synth 1 opens the Chords page (Shift + pad 25). Pad 3 (7th: C E♭ G B♭).
2. Hold Shift, tap pad 27: Pattern tools. Pad 3 (3 hits), pad 26 ([cycle](user-guide.md#cycle) 5). Rows 1–2 show the
   same group repeating: hits on steps 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15 and 16.
3. Pad 21 once (short notes). Pad 22 (soft).
4. Hold Shift, tap pad 28: Melody. Pad 4 (up-down), pad 12 (2 octaves), pad 18 (degree 1).
5. Pad 32 twice. You hear C4 E♭4 G4 B♭4 C5 E♭5 G5 B♭5 G5 E♭5: the chord sweeps up two octaves and back, in groups
   that do not fit the bar.
6. Hold Shift, tap pad 27 again: Pattern tools 2. Pad 4 (a
   [second rhythm](user-guide.md#second-rhythm-and-combine) of 4 hits: the beats 1, 5, 9 and 13).
7. Pad 24 (combine: accent). The hits on a beat (steps 1, 5 and 13) play hard, so you still hear where the bar starts.
8. Pad 26 ([thinning](user-guide.md#thinning) 1 in 3): step 13 drops out. Pad 29 (dice rhythm) turns the groups in
   the cycle and picks other gaps.

To try next: cycle 7 or 6 (Pattern tools pads 28, 27) drifts differently; Melody page pad 2 (up) makes a rising
sequence that starts again at the bottom.

## Drums

### A beat from four drums

A kick, a snare, hi-hats and a percussion part, each written by the drums'
[Pattern tools](user-guide.md#drum-pattern-tools). If your kit is different, pick a kick for Drum 1, a snare for
Drum 2 and a closed hi-hat for Drum 3 first.

1. Shift + Drum 1 opens its Repeat page. Hold Shift, tap pad 27: Pattern tools (or ▼). Pad 4 (4 hits): rows 1–2 show
   steps 1, 5, 9 and 13.
2. Pad 32 twice. The kick plays every beat: four on the floor.
3. Shift + Drum 2, then hold Shift and tap pad 27: Pattern tools. Pad 2 (2 hits): steps 1 and 9.
4. Pad 18 four times: each press moves the hits one step later, to steps 5 and 13.
5. Pad 32 twice. The snare plays on beats 2 and 4.
6. Shift + Drum 3, then Shift + pad 27: Pattern tools. Pad 8 (8 hits: every other step), pad 22 (soft).
7. Hold Shift and tap pad 27 again: Pattern tools 2 (or ▼). Pad 28 twice (white: accent on the beat).
8. Pad 32 twice. The hi-hat plays eighth notes. The ones on the beat play hard (velocity 127), the ones in between
   soft (48).
9. Shift + Drum 4, then Shift + pad 27: Pattern tools. Pad 3 (3 hits: steps 1, 7, 12), pad 18 twice (steps 3, 9, 14).
10. Pad 21 once (a roll of 2 hits a step).
11. Pad 32 twice. Each hit is now a quick double, on the 1st and 4th of the step's six micro steps.

To try next:

- Undo (pad 31) is per drum: it takes back that drum's last Write only.
- Drum 4's pad 20 (groove, bright white) picks the steps by their place in the bar instead; pad 29 (dice rhythm)
  rolls another groove.

### Hi-hats played live

[Note repeat](user-guide.md#the-repeat-page) plays a drum in time while you hold its pad: rolls, fades and triplets
by hand. It works best over a beat (the recipe above).

1. Shift + Drum 3 opens Drum 3's page. Hold Shift and tap pad 26: the Repeat page (blue). Pad 3: 1/16.
2. Hold pad 31 (it plays Drum 3): the hi-hat repeats in sixteenths, in time with the sequencer. Let go and it stops.
3. Pad 12 (accent the beat): the hits on each beat play as hard as you pressed, the others at two thirds.
4. Hold pad 31 again and, while you hold it, press pad 7 (1/16T): the repeats change to triplets at the next grid
   point. Press pad 3 to go back.
5. Pad 10 (fade): each hold starts at your press's velocity and fades to a quarter of it over one beat.
6. Pad 16 (latch): tap pad 31 and the hi-hat keeps going after you let go. Tap it again to stop it.

To try next:

- Record a roll: latch off (pad 16), then pad 15 (unquantized repeats, cyan). Press Record and hold pad 31 at 1/16T
  for a beat. The triplets record at their exact timing, even with stock's record quantise on. Each step keeps one
  velocity, so a fade records as a staircase.

## Trig conditions

### A snare roll every 4th bar, and a fill when you want it

[Trig conditions](user-guide.md#trig-conditions) let a step play only on some passes of its pattern, so a one-bar beat
changes as it repeats. Start from the beat above (A beat from four drums). Edit **stopped**: then the probability view
edits the step you tap.

1. Press Play to stop. Press Drum 2: its steps. Tap step pads 14, 15 and 16: three more snare hits (they light).
2. Shift + Pattern Settings: the probability view. Tap step pad 14. Row 4 (pads 25–32) is dark: no condition yet.
3. Hold pad 28 for half a second: a cycle of 4 passes. Pad 25 is bright cyan (pass 1), pads 26–28 dim.
4. Tap pad 28: pass 4 bright too. Tap pad 25: pass 1 dim. Step 14 now plays only on pass 4.
5. Tap step pad 15. Hold pad 28 for half a second.
6. Tap pad 28, then pad 25: step 15 plays only on pass 4.
7. Tap step pad 16. Hold pad 28 for half a second.
8. Tap pad 28, then pad 25: step 16 plays only on pass 4.
9. Press Play. Bars 1–3 are the beat as before. In bar 4 the snare rolls on steps 14, 15 and 16 into the next bar.
   Then it starts again: every 4th bar.

A fill you play by hand:

10. Press Play to stop. Press Drum 2 and tap step pads 11 and 12 (two more hits).
11. Shift + Pattern Settings, tap step pad 11. Hold Shift and tap pad 27 (FILL): it lights bright orange.
12. Tap step pad 12. Hold Shift and tap pad 27 (FILL).
13. Press Drum 1 and Shift + Pattern Settings. Tap step pad 13. Hold Shift and tap pad 28 (NOT FILL): the kick on
    beat 4 drops out during a fill.
14. Press Play. Steps 11 and 12 stay silent, the kick plays beat 4.
15. Press Drum 2, then Note: the expanded view. Hold pads 21 and 22 together (FILL for Drums 1 and 2, bright orange):
    the snare plays steps 11 and 12 and the kick skips beat 4. Let go and the beat comes back.

To try next:

- Tap pad 22 instead of holding it: FILL is armed (yellow) and fills Drum 2's whole next pass, then turns off by
  itself.
- Hold Shift on row 4 for the other specials: FIRST plays a step only on the first pass after Play, PRE plays it only
  when the track's last conditional step played (pass 1 of 2 at 50 % probability, then PRE: a pair that plays
  together or not at all).
