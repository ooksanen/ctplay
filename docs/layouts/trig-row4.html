CTPlay PL20 · trig conditions · sketch

Three ways to fill row 4 of the probability view

Shift + Pattern Settings opens stock's probability view. Rows 1–2 pick the step, row 3 is stock's probability. Row 4 is ignored by stock: the condition goes there. Pads are drawn as on the device, 8 per row; colours are rough.

Same in every option. One condition per step, on drums and on Synth / MIDI tracks. Stock's probability still applies, so “1:4 at 50 %” works. Each pattern counts its own passes; the count starts over when you press Play, pick a pattern, chain or scene, or load a project, and keeps counting while a chain plays itself.

A

Divider pads

closest to Elektron

Four pads choose the cycle (÷2, ÷3, ÷4, ÷8); tapping again steps through which pass plays. Three pads for the special conditions. Shift turns any of them into its “not”.

Good

  • Covers the conditions musicians use most (1:2 … 4:4, 8:8, FIRST, FILL, PRE) with their negations.
  • Every condition is one or two taps away.
  • Leaves pad 32 free (LAST later).

Costs

  • No cycles of 5, 6 or 7.
  • Which pass a ÷ pad plays is hidden until you hold the pad.
  • Only fixed “A of B” values, not “passes 1 and 3 of 4”.

Storage · Drums: 8 bits (one byte per step). Notes: 8 of their 16 bits.

B

Pass grid

most visual, fewest features

Row 4 is the passes themselves. Shift + a pad sets the cycle length; a tap ticks the passes the step plays on. Nothing else on the row.

Good

  • What you see is what plays: the lit passes are the ones that sound.
  • Any mix of passes, cycles 2–8 (covers A:B and “not A:B” without extra values).
  • One idea to learn.

Costs

  • No FILL, FIRST or PRE: they would need a home somewhere else, later.
  • Shift is spent on the cycle length, so it can't be a modifier for anything else here.

Storage · Drums: 9 bits (the byte plus one shared bit). Notes: 11 of 16 bits.

C

Pass grid + Shift layer

recommended

Option B's pass grid, with the cycle length set by holding a pass pad for ½ s. Holding Shift swaps row 4 for the special conditions: FIRST, FILL, PRE, LAST and their “not”s, one pad each.

Good

  • Everything from A and B: any pass mix, cycles 2–8, and all the special conditions.
  • Both layers are one pad per value, as every CTPlay page.
  • Shift as a layer switch matches CTPlay's Quick view habit.

Costs

  • A step has a pass cycle or a special condition, not both (the drums' 9 bits).
  • The ½ s hold is a new gesture in CTPlay.
  • LAST needs the next pattern known in advance: may stay dark at first.

Storage · Drums: 9 bits (pass masks use most codes, the 8 specials fit in the rest). Notes: 16 bits, room left.
