CTPlay voice log (PL03D)

Chrome or Edge, the Circuit Tracks connected over USB with PL03D installed. Play, then Read: one row per synth note, oldest first (the device keeps the last 12). Copy as text puts the table on the clipboard to paste.

took = what the chosen voice was doing: free, sounding (a note still playing or fading: the loss we are looking for) or starting (allocated by this same press, not started yet). x threshold below 1.00 means the DSP picked the voice, unless the path says ARM (floor): CTPlay let a chord note take a free voice. voices before: the synth's 6 voices just before (- free, r sounding, H starting).
